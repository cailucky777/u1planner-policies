Privacy Policy

Updated: November 3, 2026

Your journal on this device

Journal pages, handwriting, photos, stamps, custom artwork, cover settings, tape patterns and activity days are saved on your device. Activity days support personal in-app statistics and keepsakes. Opening the app in the foreground counts each local day once.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. When enabled, journal content, notebook types, revision information, cover decorations and usage dates are stored through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database and use your iCloud storage. Revision, time and account identifiers manage updates and account separation. Your journal is not sent to developer-operated servers.

Purchases and Pro access

Apple and RevenueCat verify purchases, restore purchases and manage Pro access. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous customer identifier, transaction verification data, purchase and subscription status, and necessary device/app version context for access, fraud prevention and purchase analytics. We do not send journal text, photos, handwriting or calendar content to RevenueCat, or set name, email or advertising-identifier attributes. Journal iCloud data is not used as proof of purchase.

Photos and permissions

The app uses the system photo picker and imports only photos you select. It does not request your entire photo library, location, contacts or microphone.

Optional system calendars

Only after you connect and authorize calendar access does the app read selected calendars. Adding, changing or deleting events through the system editor writes to the corresponding calendar and its provider may sync the change. Events are displayed in memory and are not stored in journal iCloud data. You may choose to include event snapshots in an export.

Optional image generation

Photo cropping, subject extraction and pixel conversion run on the device. Optional AI redraw uses Apple Image Playground when you open it. The system may offer external model options; review the provider and data information shown by Apple. U1planner does not operate an image-generation server. Only results you select are saved.

Export and sharing

Images and PDFs are generated on your device. Only when you save or share do files go to your selected destination or app; recipients may handle them under their own privacy policies. Exports may contain journal content and are outside U1planner’s system app lock.

Ads, analytics and external links

This version contains no ads or cross-app advertising tracking and does not use your journal to train AI. RevenueCat provides purchase verification and purchase analytics as described above. Apple and RevenueCat process service data under their own privacy policies. External websites such as YouTube open when you tap a link and follow their own policies.

Managing and deleting content

You can delete page content in the app. Turning off sync does not delete local or cloud content. Earlier content can remain in retained revisions. To clear the cloud journal, first turn off sync on every device, then manage the app’s data in iCloud storage settings. Handle each device’s local copies separately; enabling sync may upload local content again. Deleting the app removes its local data on that device but does not automatically delete iCloud data, cancel an Apple subscription or remove retained purchase-verification records. For purchase data requests, contact the developer; transaction records may be retained as needed for legal and purchase-verification purposes.

Contact the developer
