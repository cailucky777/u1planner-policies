Privacy Policy

Updated: October 30, 2026

Your journal on this device

Journal pages, handwriting, photos, stamps, custom artwork, cover settings and activity days are saved on your device. Activity days power your personal in-app statistics, not advertising analytics.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. When enabled, your journal content and its revision information are stored through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database and use your iCloud storage. Sync uses revision, time and account identifiers to manage updates and account separation. The app does not send your journal to developer-operated servers.

Photos & permissions

The app uses the system photo picker and imports only photos you select. It does not request access to your entire photo library, location, contacts or microphone.

Export & sharing

Images and PDFs are generated on this device. Files are passed to your chosen destination or app only when you choose to save or share them; the recipient may handle them under its own privacy policy. Exports may include your journal content and are outside U1planner’s system app lock.

Ads, analytics & external links

This version includes no ads, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking components, and does not use your journal to train AI. Apple handles service data under its own privacy policy. External sites such as YouTube open only when you tap a link and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Managing & deleting content

You can delete content from pages in the app. Turning off sync does not delete local or cloud content. Retained sync revisions may still contain earlier content. To clear the whole cloud journal, first turn off sync on all devices, then manage this app’s data in Apple’s iCloud storage settings. Local copies on other devices must be handled separately; enabling sync again may upload local content again. Deleting the app removes its local app data on that device but does not automatically clear iCloud data.

Contact the developer

For privacy questions, contact Luying Cai (Savina) using the contact details on the developer website. This policy will be updated to reflect changes to the app.
